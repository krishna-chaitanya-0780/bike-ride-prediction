02 — DATA SIGNALS

What shapes a fare.

01 / WEATHER

Tomorrow matters.

Open-Meteo hourly rainfall, temperature and humidity provide real forecasts up to the planning horizon. Short rides prioritize near-term hours.

02 / ROAD ROUTE

Not straight-line distance.

Road travel distance and untrafficked travel time can come from OSRM during development. A line on a map is not a priced provider route.

03 / HISTORICAL PRICES

Train on real paid fares.

Only lawful historical observations can train the future-price model. A temporal test split reports INR error and prevents unsupported accuracy claims.

04 / TRAFFIC

Current is not future.

TomTom's optional licensed endpoint can observe current flow. Future surge, congestion and driver supply cannot be guaranteed seven days ahead.

03 — CONNECTOR TRANSPARENCY

One plan. Three providers.

Uber and Ola developer interfaces require provider approval and authorization. No verified public Rapido fare API is connected. We show provider availability honestly.
